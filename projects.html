Electronics & Logic

Smart Chicken Coop

Problem: Small-scale residential chicken farming faces unstable coop temperatures, ammonia odors, inconsistent lighting, and heavy manual monitoring, which can reduce laying hen productivity and disturb nearby residents.
Role: Structured and documented the project's technical specifications
Tools: ESP32 development board (with built-in Wi-Fi), DHT22 temperature sensor, MQ-137 ammonia sensor,Real Time Clock (RTC) module.
Outcome: The project delivers an automated coop system that regulates lighting/heating and ammonia mist spraying with manual button overrides, provides local LCD and remote Google Sheets/Looker Studio monitoring, and improves hen welfare, egg yield, and nearby air quality, with tank-level refill alerts planned for future enhancement.

COBOT

Problem: Automated headlamp installation cells face safety hazards such as cobot contact, conveyor pinch/jam risks, and electrical or mechanical faults, requiring structured pre-start checks, I/O verification, and sequence testing before full operation.
Role: Structured and documented the project's technical specifications
Tools: TM5-700 industrial robot & controller box, HMI interface, Omron PLC CJ2M-CPU31-1P with PA202, MAD42, ID211, and OC211 modules.
Outcome: The commissioning workflow validates equipment readiness, risk controls, mechanical/electrical interfaces, and PLC/HMI sequences for right and left headlamp VAC installation, while flagging a hydraulic cooling leak at Workstations 3 and 4 and swapped HMI 1/HMI 2 IP addresses for resolution.
